### 1. Nature of the Service
*elichingon.com* offers an Artificial Intelligence (AI) assisted interpretation service designed to translate the archaic and symbolic language of the canonical I Ching texts into clear language tailored to the context provided by the user. The AI acts as a tool for textual translation and analysis, and its responses do not constitute professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice under any circumstances.

### 2. Credits and Gift Card System
* The use of AI interpretation consumes **1 credit** per consultation successfully processed by the orchestrator.
* Credits are enabled through valid and active *Gift Card* codes issued by the platform's administration or trusted third parties.
* During the current testing phase (closed Beta), access codes are distributed free of charge upon direct request via email to `dailingna@elichingon.com`.

### 3. Privacy and Data Handling
* The questions and detailed contexts you input are securely processed through our technological infrastructure and associated models under strict confidentiality standards.
* We do not store personal identity profiles linked to your casts; logs are strictly limited to operational and transactional metrics aimed at balance and credit control.
